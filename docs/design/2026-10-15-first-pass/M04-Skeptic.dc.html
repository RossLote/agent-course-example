Finding. If the learner drops a second item while a wrong-placement message is still on screen, the drop is ignored: the item snaps back to the tray with no message.

The skeptic's rules

01

Confirmed means you read the lines and the scenario happens.

02

Refuted only by citing the exact line that makes it impossible. No line, no refutation.

03

Uncertain is allowed. It keeps the finding alive at lower priority. A wrong refutation costs a bug; an uncertain costs minutes.

04

"Unlikely", "a test exists", "the framework handles it", "the spec says so": none of these is a refutation.
