What to watch for

Read the agent's reply twice. Count the things you did not ask for. Count the things it decided. Notice who tested it.

What just happened

1

It filled every gap with a guess. Agents do not stop at a gap; they fill it, and they sound sure when they do. The timer came from a gap you left.

2

It built more than you asked. Given room, an agent builds into it. More is not better; more is more to check.

3

It marked its own homework. The agent that built it is the worst judge of it. It believes its own plan.

4

Here is the part that works. The same agent, pointed at someone else's work with a clear question, is sharp. That asymmetry is the whole workflow: box the builder, then let the reviewers loose.
