The epic

The fridge activity, cut into pieces

Eight decisions become five beads. Each says what it is, which decisions bind it, what it may touch, and how a reviewer will know it is done. The demo script on the right is what done means for the whole thing.

.1 The data runs alone

Shelves, items, rules and reasons in one file, from the client's guide (D2).

Acceptance: a test that every item has exactly one right shelf and a reason.

.2 The fridge and dragging runs alone · sets the pattern

Four shelves, eight items, drag with mouse and touch; a right drop stays, a wrong drop returns (D3, D5). Depends on .1.

Acceptance: drag raw chicken to the top shelf: it returns; to the bottom shelf: it stays.

.3 Keyboard and screen reader with .4

Enter, arrows, Enter; announcements of item, shelf and result (D5). Depends on .2.

Acceptance: you write it below.

.4 Feedback and the score screen with .3

The one-line reason on a wrong drop (D3); the score screen and Try again (D4, D7). Depends on .2.

Touches: the feedback and score files only.

.5 The walkthrough last

Runs the demo script in a browser, recorded, one chapter per step. Depends on .3 and .4.

order: .1, then .2, then .3 and .4 together (they touch different files), then .5.

The demo script (done means)

  1. Open the activity: four labelled shelves and eight items in a tray.
  2. Drag raw chicken to the top shelf: it returns, and the message reads "Raw meat and fish go on the bottom shelf so they can't drip onto other food."
  3. Drag raw chicken to the bottom shelf: it stays.
  4. Tab to the lettuce, Enter, arrow down to the salad drawer, Enter: placed; the screen reader says "Lettuce placed in the salad drawer. Correct."
  5. Place the other six; the score screen reads "7 out of 8 right first time" and lists raw chicken with its reason.
  6. Press Try again: the tray is full and the score is cleared.

Every step performed in a browser and recorded, with all the checks green. Not "the agent says so".
