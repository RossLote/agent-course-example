The one thing to remember

Agents are better at reviewing than building.

"So whenever you have agents do anything, also have agents look at what the agents have done. Put the agent in a tight box it can't wander off from, and check, and check again."

Ross Lote

Every module adds one wall to the box on the right. By the end you can set up a piece of agent work so that you can trust what comes back.
