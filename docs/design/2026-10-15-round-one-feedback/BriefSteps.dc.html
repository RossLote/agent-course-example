How a brief goes

  1. 1

    You describe it freely first. A paragraph in your own words, however rough.

  2. 2

    The agent reads before it asks. What exists already, the client's materials, the standard if there is one. Then it asks.

  3. 3

    At most five questions a round, ranked. The question whose answer settles the most others comes first. Each comes with what it decides and the agent's recommended answer, so "your defaults, except number 3" is a complete reply.

  4. 4

    You answer in a paragraph. The agent plays back what it took each answer to be, re-asks only what you missed, and asks the next round.

  5. 5

    It stops when the rest is a builder's call. Then you get the decisions: D1, D2, D3…, one sentence each with its reason, on one record the builder reads.

"You may not know what you want until you see the first round. The brief is where you find out what you do know."

Ross
